Strategic Academic Initiatives

ICMTA Member Directory

The Indian Commerce Management Teachers Faculty Directory serves as a comprehensive and vital resource, systematically cataloging the academic professionals who form the backbone of commerce and management education across the nation. Our directory aggregates essential professional details including qualifications, specializations, research, teaching experience, and professional affiliations.

Explore Member Directory →

ICMTA Careers

ICMTA Careers is a dedicated academic career initiative established to support, connect, and empower educators, researchers, scholars, and academic professionals in identifying and accessing faculty career opportunities across educational institutions. It serves as a bridge between institutions seeking qualified faculty and talented educators aspiring for meaningful academic careers.

GPSN Communication Forum

A global community committed to empowering individuals through the art of public speaking and effective communication. It provides educators with a supportive peer stage to practice presentation mastery, lecture delivery, and academic storytelling.

Policy Pulse

Policy Pulse is a structured, asynchronous knowledge-sharing initiative designed to demystify the ever-changing regulatory landscape. Instead of expecting members to read lengthy legal texts, this activity distils complex updates into digestible, actionable insights for educators.
