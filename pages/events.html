Time 7:00 PM (IST)
Platform / Mode Zoom • Online
Registration Fee Zero Registration Fees (Free)

ALERT - 2026: “Empowering Educators with Future Skills”

Date 30th September 2026 (Wednesday)
Time 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (IST)
Mode / Platform Online
Registration Fee No Registration Fee (Free)
Organized by Indian Commerce and Management Teachers Association (ICMTA)
In Association with Sri Krishnadevaraya University, Anantapuram - 515003, Andhra Pradesh
Theme & Pillars People · Pedagogy · Research · Impact  |  Educators Shape Better Tomorrows

The Indian Commerce and Management Teachers Association (ICMTA), in association with Sri Krishnadevaraya University, Anantapuram, organizes a One Day Online Faculty Development Programme (FDP) on ALERT - 2026: "Empowering Educators with Future Skills".

ALERT-2026 is designed to empower educators with future-ready skills for excellence in higher education. The programme offers a meaningful platform for teachers and academic leaders to strengthen their professional capabilities, enrich teaching-learning practices, and enhance research orientation. It focuses on key areas such as Academic Leadership, Learning Excellence, Research Innovation, and Transformative Practices.

FDP Focus & Highlights

  • Academic Leadership: Institutional Vision & Strategy
  • Learning Excellence: Pedagogy & Student Engagement
  • Research Innovation: Methodologies & Publications
  • Transformative Practices: Future Skills for Educators

Two-Day National Seminar

“Ethical Leadership and Management through the Lens of Bhagavad Gita for Sustainable Development towards Viksit Bharat @ 2047”

Date 3rd & 4th November 2026
Mode Offline
Venue Siddhartha Academy of Higher Education (Deemed to be University), Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh
Organized by School of Management, Siddhartha Academy of Higher Education
In association with AIMS & Chinmaya Mission

The seminar aims to provide a platform for academicians, researchers, industry professionals and students to share their research, experiences and insights on the relevance of the Bhagavad Gita to contemporary management and ethical leadership.

The seminar focuses on ethical leadership, management, strategic leadership, corporate sustainability, governance, youth leadership, ecology, economy and ethics, and sustainable development towards Viksit Bharat @ 2047.
